    Method

    How this map is made

    • Built from public announcements: company and government releases, and reputable trade and business publications. Every link cites its source.
    • Not exhaustive. It shows the deals that have been announced publicly and found, not every deal.
    • These are announced investments and partnerships, not official foreign direct investment (FDI) statistics, which are compiled differently, for example by Statistics Canada.
    • A link is a documented public deal. It is not an endorsement of ArchangX, and it does not mean the investor or the company has any relationship with ArchangX.
    • Included: an investor or partner headquartered outside Canada, and a company headquartered or mainly operating in Canada in satellite and space, defence, dual-use or wildfire technology, or AI. Equity and partnerships are recorded separately, an MOU is labelled as an MOU, and a deal that ended stays on the map, marked as ended, with its date.
    • Updated after every quarter. To send a correction or a missing deal, email shahin.vakilinia@archangx.com with a link to a public source.
