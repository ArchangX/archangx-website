Privacy policy

How ArchAngX Corp. (“ArchangX”, “we”) collects, uses, shares and protects your personal information. Last updated 24 November 2026.

In short

This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics or trackers. We collect personal information only when you register for an event, buy a ticket, fill in one of our forms on LinkedIn or another event platform, or write to us. We use it to answer you and to run our events. We do not sell it.

1. Who is responsible

ArchAngX Corp., a Canadian company, is responsible for the personal information described here. The person in charge of the protection of personal information is Shahin Vakilinia, reachable at shahin.vakilinia@archangx.com. Write to this address for any question, request or complaint about your personal information.

2. What we collect and why

Visiting this website

We do not use cookies, analytics, advertising tags, tracking pixels, or fonts and scripts from other companies, and we receive no information about who visits. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; like any web host, GitHub records technical data such as your IP address in its server logs to run and secure the service. We have no access to those logs. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Registering for an event on this website

The registration form for Metatron Summit 2027 asks for your full name, work or university email, organisation, job title or role, phone number, city, the days you plan to attend and whether you will join the evening celebration, and optionally your LinkedIn profile and any interests, accessibility or dietary needs. We use these details to confirm your place, check eligibility for free registration, plan seating, catering and accessibility, prepare badges and check-in, and send you information about the event you registered for. The form is delivered to our email by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a form-forwarding service.

Buying a ticket

Tickets and exhibition booths are paid on checkout pages run by Stripe. Stripe collects your card details; we never see or store your full card number. Stripe passes us the order details: your name, email, billing address and the amount paid, plus what the checkout asks for, such as the names of other attendees, accessibility or dietary needs, a school or institution for student passes, and a company name and phone number for booths. We use them to issue your ticket and receipt, prepare the attendee list, badges and check-in, handle refunds and keep our accounting and tax records. See the Stripe privacy policy.

Forms on LinkedIn and other event platforms

When you submit one of our forms on LinkedIn (a lead generation form) or register for one of our events on an event platform such as Luma, the platform shares with us the details you submit, typically your name, email, organisation, job title and the answers to the form's questions. We use them to answer your request, send the information you asked for and run the event you registered for. The platform's own privacy policy covers what it collects itself; see the LinkedIn privacy policy.

Writing to us

The contact form on this website does not send anything: it opens a pre-filled email in your own email app. When you email us, we receive your address and what you write, and use them to reply. Our email runs on Google Workspace.

Accessibility and dietary needs

These details can reveal information about health or religion. Give them only if you want us to accommodate them. We use them only for that purpose and share them only with the venue or caterer when needed to meet them.

3. Consent and the emails we send

By submitting a form or buying a ticket, you agree to the uses described above for that form or ticket. We email you about what you asked for: your registration, ticket or request. We send news about other events or our work only if you agreed to receive it, for example by ticking a consent box on a form, and you can withdraw that consent at any time by replying “unsubscribe” or writing to us. Withdrawing consent does not affect what was done before, and we may still need to contact you about a ticket you bought.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information, and we do not give attendee lists to sponsors, exhibitors or other partners without your consent. We share personal information only with:

  • the service providers that run these services for us: GitHub (website hosting), FormSubmit (form delivery), Stripe (payments), Google Workspace (email and documents), and LinkedIn or event platforms when you use their forms;
  • an event venue or caterer, limited to what is needed for your accessibility or dietary needs and for access to the venue;
  • authorities, when the law requires it.

5. Where it is stored

Our service providers may store and process personal information outside Québec and outside Canada, including in the United States. While it is there, it is subject to the laws of that country, and authorities there may be able to access it.

6. How long we keep it

  • Event registrations, attendee lists and accessibility or dietary needs: deleted no later than 12 months after the event, unless you agreed to hear about future events (then your name and email are kept until you withdraw).
  • Payment and order records: kept as long as Canadian tax law requires, currently six years.
  • Emails and form requests: kept as long as needed to answer you and for our business records.

7. How we protect it

This website has no database, user accounts or server code, so it stores no personal information. What we receive is kept in our business email and document accounts, with access limited to the people who need it to answer you or run the event. If a privacy incident creates a risk of serious harm, we will tell the people affected and report it to the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec or the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, as the law requires.

8. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you and give you a copy, including in a structured, commonly used technology format;
  • correct information that is wrong or incomplete;
  • stop using it, or delete it, when we no longer need it or you withdraw your consent;
  • explain how we collected and used it.

Write to shahin.vakilinia@archangx.com. We may ask you to confirm your identity, and we answer within 30 days, free of charge. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec or the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

9. Children

This website and our forms are not meant for children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you think a child under 14 has sent us information, write to us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

When our practices change, we update this page and the date at the top. If a change affects how we use information you have already given us, we will tell you before it applies.
